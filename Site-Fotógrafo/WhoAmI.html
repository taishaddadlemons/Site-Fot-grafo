    • My story with photography began when I was just 3 years old. My beloved grandmother gave me a toy camera, and since then, my passion for the art and profession of photography has only grown stronger.

      What started as playful curiosity soon turned into a lifelong pursuit of capturing moments, emotions, and stories through the lens. With every photo taken, I discovered a new way to see the world and express myself creatively.

      Over the years, my dedication to photography deepened—I studied different techniques, experimented with lighting and composition, and learned how to tell powerful stories without saying a word. Photography became more than a hobby; it became my calling and my profession.

      Today, I continue to strive to capture the beauty in everyday life, preserving memories that will last forever. This journey that began with a simple toy camera shaped who I am and fuels my passion every day.

      Specialization: Nature Photography
      Experience: 8 years of experience capturing the beauty of nature and authentic moments of people in natural settings.
      Style: A style that combines spontaneity with natural aesthetics, creating images that tell stories and evoke emotions.
      Mission: "To capture the essence of nature and human beauty in harmony with the environment, inspiring people to appreciate the world around them."
      Education: Graduated in Photography from the University of Visual Arts at Capilano and participated in nature photography workshops with renowned international photographers.
